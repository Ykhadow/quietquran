Two ways to read

Read the way that suits your eyes.

Switch at any time. Your place is kept by ayah, so you never lose it.

IndoPak or Madani

The script you learned from.

The Nastaliq of South Asia's Mushafs, or the Uthmani script of the Madinah Mushaf. Choose yours, and switch whenever you like.

Seven page layouts, each line for line as printed: IndoPak 15, 16, 13 and 9-line, and the Madinah Mushaf.

Sessions

Keep several readings going at once.

A daily reading, a surah you're memorising, Al-Kahf on Fridays. Each one resumes where you left it, or starts its surah afresh.

Sources and credits

Where everything comes from.

The Quran text, its page layouts and the translations come from the Quranic Universal Library, a proofread source. Printed pages are from the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex in Madinah, and recitations from EveryAyah.

Every source, font and contributor is credited on the app's Sources page.
